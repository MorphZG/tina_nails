Autumn shades worth wearing

Deep plum, soft mocha and milky rose are leading the season.

Read more

Rich, muted tones flatter every skin tone and pair with knitwear beautifully. Try a sheer rose for work, a plum cherry for evenings, and add a fine gold line for celebrations. Ask us about shades that suit your undertone.

August 2026

How to make your gel last longer

Four habits that protect your set between visits.

Read more

Wear gloves for washing up, apply cuticle oil daily, never pick or peel, and use your fingertips rather than nails to open things. Book an infill every three to four weeks to keep your set balanced.

July 2026

The five-minute hand ritual

A tiny evening habit for softer hands and a calmer mind.

Read more

Warm a little hand cream between your palms, press gently into each finger, and breathe slowly. Finish with oil on the cuticles. It takes five minutes and feels like a mini spa visit.
